An invoice approval workflow is the structured path an invoice travels from receipt through review, approval, payment authorization, and reconciliation. When a finance team is small, that path can live in someone's inbox and still work. As invoice volume climbs and more departments, vendors, and locations enter the picture, an informal process starts to leak time, money, and control.
