Key takeaways Accounts payable reconciliation proves that recorded liabilities are complete, accurate, and supported. It compares the AP subsidiary ledger, the general ledger control account, vendor records, and payment activity as of one cutoff date. A balanced control account is not proof of a clean AP ledger.
