Key takeaways Accounts receivable automation software handles invoice delivery, payment collection, reminders, tracking, and reconciliation so finance teams stop chasing payments by hand. Manual AR is often the hidden cause of late payments. Atradius found that administrative inefficiencies in the payment process are the top reason US B2B invoices go overdue.
